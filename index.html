Frequently asked questions

About us

Is eam e-work a real company?
Yes. We're part of eam productions, a Chandigarh audio, video and translation studio with a public portfolio at eam-ework.com. Registration: [legal name, reg. no.].
Who is the work for?
Companies that build AI tools such as voice assistants, translation apps and photo recognition. They need real examples from real people to train them.

Getting started

What do I need to join?
You need to be 18 or older and have a smartphone. Some projects also need a laptop, home WiFi, a particular language or some experience, and each gig says so clearly.
I've never done work like this. Is that okay?
Yes. Photo and most voice gigs come with simple step-by-step instructions. Anyone can do them.
Which countries can join?
We run projects in more than 14 countries, including India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Taiwan, Kenya, Ghana, Mexico, Colombia, Peru and Chile. New countries are added as projects open, so tell us where you live when you join.

The work

How long does a gig take?
Most photo and voice gigs take under an hour. Transcription, calling and voice-over projects run longer, and the brief tells you the time involved.
Will there be more work after my first gig?
New projects open every month. Once you've joined, we contact you whenever one matches your profile.

Pay

How much can I earn?
Short photo and voice gigs usually pay around ₹400 (about US$5) each. Skilled work is priced per project. You always see the rate before you start.
