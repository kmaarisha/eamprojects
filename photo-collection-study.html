About the study

Faces change over the years. New hairstyles, glasses, weight changes and natural ageing all alter how a person looks, and technology that verifies identity must still identify the same person reliably. This study pairs your recent photos with older ones, giving these systems genuine examples of how appearance evolves over time.

The study is run by eam (GSTIN 04ALHPS6037K2ZS), based in Chandigarh, India. Photographs are delivered to a technology client without your name or contact details, kept for up to one year and then deleted. Full details are in our privacy policy.

  • Only your face is neededYou submit photographs of your face alone.
  • Used for this study onlyYour photographs are used solely to develop and test the technology described here. They are never sold, published or used in advertising.
  • Your choice at every stepTaking part is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any point before your submission is complete, and your photographs will not be used.

What you will submit

Thirty photographs in total, uploaded through the EAM DataCo app.

20 photos

Past photos

Existing photographs of yourself, selected from your gallery.

  • Begin with your oldest photographs, ideally five years old or more, and progress to recent ones.
  • At least one image must be 12 months old or older.
  • Each image must be taken at least one day apart.
  • Each image should show a different background and different clothing.
6 photos

Current photos

New photographs of yourself, all taken on the same day.

  • You can wear the same clothing in every image.
  • Use a different background each time, such as a different wall or room.
  • Maintain a neutral expression, without smiling.
4 photos

Guided head-pose photos

Captured directly in the app, which guides each pose.

  • Look up, down, left and right when the app prompts you.
  • The same clothing and background may be used for all four.

Photo guidelines

Every submission is reviewed against these standards. Following them helps your photos get approved the first time.

✓Required in every photo

  • Look directly at the camera
  • Keep your eyes clearly visible
  • Only you, in every photo
  • Whole face inside the frame
  • Genuine, original photos

✕The following will be disqualified

  • AI-generated or duplicate images
  • Heavy filters or editing
  • Heavy make-up or face paint
  • Reflections on glasses or eyes
  • Other people in the frame
  • Caps, hats or other headwear
  • Hands, hair or shadows on the face
  • Blurred, rotated or cropped photos
  • Cropped images
  • Files below 128 KB
